Privacy Policy

Milestone: Goals & To-Do List · Last updated 8 October 2026

Milestone is an offline app. It has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. This policy is short because there is very little to describe.

What Milestone collects

Nothing. The developer of Milestone receives no data about you or your use of the app — not your goals, not your reflections, not usage statistics, not crash reports, not an advertising identifier.

What Milestone stores, and where

Everything you type into Milestone — goals, milestones, steps, life areas, seasons, weekly reflections and your display name — is written to a database that lives inside the app's private storage on your own device. It is not transmitted anywhere.

That data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data from Android Settings. Because it is only on your device, the developer cannot recover it for you.

Does the app connect to the internet?

No. Milestone does not request the INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not allow it to open a network connection at all. The app works identically in flight mode.

The permissions it does request are:

PermissionWhy
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To show the optional weekly reflection reminder. Nothing else is ever posted.
WAKE_LOCK, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Required by Android's WorkManager library, which schedules that one weekly reminder so it survives a reboot. Despite its name, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE only lets the library read whether a network exists; it grants no network access.

Backups you create yourself

Milestone can export your data as a single file. This only ever happens when you tap "Export a backup". The app then hands that file to Android's share sheet, and you choose where it goes — a cloud drive, a messaging app, a folder on your phone.

Once you hand the file to another app, that app's own privacy policy governs it. Milestone has no involvement in, and no knowledge of, where it ends up. The backup file is plain, unencrypted JSON, so treat it as you would any personal document.

Children

Milestone is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Third parties

Milestone contains no advertising, analytics, attribution, crash-reporting or tracking libraries of any kind. No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it does with your data, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the date above will change.

A goal you only ever wrote down is a wish. Milestone turns it into a path you can actually walk: a handful of milestones, one next step, and a quiet weekly look back at how it went.

No account. No sync. No ads. Everything you write stays on your phone.

WHAT IT DOES

• Set a goal, and say why it matters. The "why" sits at the top of the goal, where you will see it on the days you would rather not bother.

• Break it into milestones. Milestones are the real unit of progress — not a checkbox list of chores, but the few points that mean you have genuinely moved.

• Break a milestone down further when it feels too big. Steps are optional; turn them off entirely if a plain goal-and-milestone view suits you better.

• See one next step at a time. The home screen shows the single thing to do next, not a backlog that makes you feel behind before you start.

• Follow the Life Map. Each goal is drawn as a path with your position on it, so a long project looks like distance travelled rather than a percentage.

• Look back every week. Five short questions — what moved, what didn't, what surprised you, what you are proud of, what comes next. Two minutes, no scoring, no streak to break.

• Read your past weeks whenever you like. Reflections are kept in your own words, exactly as you wrote them.

• Close a chapter honestly. Goals that are no longer right can be set down with a reason rather than deleted and quietly forgotten. Past Chapters keeps them, and you can pick one up again whenever.

• Organise by life area and by season — Health, Career, Knowledge, whatever you name them — so a few months of focus is a thing you can see.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Milestone has no servers, because there is nothing for a server to do. Your goals, your milestones and your reflections are written to this phone and never leave it. The app works exactly the same in flight mode.

It does not ask for an email address. It cannot read your contacts, your location or your files. There is no analytics, no tracking and no advertising SDK.

A backup is the one way data leaves, and only when you ask: export writes a single file and hands it to whichever app you choose — your own cloud drive, a message to yourself, a folder on your phone. Restoring from that file puts everything back as it was.

THE WEEKLY NUDGE

One optional reminder, on a Sunday evening, to sit down with the week. It stays quiet if you have already written that week's reflection. That is the only notification the app will ever send you.

NO STREAKS, NO SCORES

There is no streak to protect and no number grading your week. A goal is shown as moving steadily, needing attention, or stalled, and that is as judgemental as it gets. The point is to notice honestly, not to perform.

WORKS FOR

Fitness and training plans. Learning a language or an instrument. Writing a book. Launching a side project. Saving for something. Reading more. Any goal with a finish line and a few stages on the way there.
